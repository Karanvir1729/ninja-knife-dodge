# Privacy Policy — Ninja Knife Dodge

_Last updated: July 29, 2026_

**Ninja Knife Dodge does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- The game runs entirely offline and makes no network connections.
- There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Your high score and tutorial progress are saved only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or opt out of.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
