# Privacy policy - Ninja Knife Dodge

**Ninja Knife Dodge keeps one account per player so that seals, chapters and best scores follow you between devices.**

## Your account

- You sign in with Apple. Apple gives the game a random account identifier and, if you allow it, your email address (Apple lets you hide it behind a relay address). Nothing else from your Apple ID is shared with us.
- After each round the game backs up your ninja name, best scores, chapter and seal progress and boosters to the game's account database, hosted by [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) in the United States. Your ninja name and best scores may be shown on leaderboards; your email address is never shown to other players and is not used for marketing.
- You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Account inside the game. This erases the cloud copy immediately and permanently. Deleting the app deletes the copy on your device.
- The game itself plays offline; it makes no other connections and has no analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Ads

The App Store version is ad supported. Two kinds of ad can appear, both served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and general usage information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy):

- **Between rounds**, a short full-screen ad may play when you leave a results screen. It never interrupts play or a tutorial, never appears in the first rounds of a session, shows at most once every few rounds, and never twice within a couple of minutes.
- **Rewarded videos** you choose to watch in exchange for a power-up, a hint, a level skip or a second chance. Every reward can also be earned or used without watching one.

The app never asks to track you and never reads the advertising identifier, so the ads are non-personalised.

To access or correct the data held for your account, or to ask a question, email mehar.khanna@uwaterloo.ca; to delete it, use Settings > Account in the game.
