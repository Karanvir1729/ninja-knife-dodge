# Privacy Policy — Ninja Knife Dodge

_Last updated: September 1, 2026_

**Ninja Knife Dodge does not collect, store, or share any personal data itself.**

- The game runs offline and makes no network connections of its own.
- There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Your scores, leaderboards, level progress, power-ups, settings, ninja name and tutorial progress are saved only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Optional rewarded ads

The game never shows ads on its own. In some versions you can **choose** to watch a short rewarded video in exchange for a power-up, a hint, a level skip or a second chance. When you tap "Watch ad", the ad is served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and general usage information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). On iOS you will be asked whether to allow tracking; if you decline, ads are non-personalised. Every reward can also be earned or used without watching an ad, and versions built without the ad module never load an ad and collect no data at all.

Because the game itself collects no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or opt out of.
