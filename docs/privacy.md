# Privacy Policy — Ninja Knife Dodge

_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

**Ninja Knife Dodge does not collect, store, or share any personal data itself.**

- The game runs offline and makes no network connections of its own.
- There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Your scores, leaderboards, level progress, power-ups, settings, ninja name and tutorial progress are saved only on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Ads

The App Store version is ad supported. Two kinds of ad can appear, both served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and general usage information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy):

- **Between rounds**, a short full-screen ad may play when you leave a results screen. It never interrupts play or a tutorial, never appears in the first rounds of a session, shows at most once every few rounds, and never twice within a couple of minutes.
- **Rewarded videos** you **choose** to watch in exchange for a power-up, a hint, a level skip or a second chance. Every reward can also be earned or used without watching one.

The app never asks to track you and never reads the advertising identifier, so the ads are non-personalised. Versions built without the ad module never load an ad and collect no data at all.

Because the game itself collects no data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete, or opt out of.
